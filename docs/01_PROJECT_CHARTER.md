# 01 — Project Charter

**Status:** APPROVED (founding)  
**Authority:** Highest governing document for purpose and scope

## Purpose

Define what Muscular America is, who it serves, and what it explicitly refuses to become.

## What belongs here

- Mission and scope
- Audience
- Platform inclusions and exclusions
- Non-negotiable boundaries (medical, ethical, editorial)

## What does not belong here

- Directory maps (see Repository Architecture)
- Visual tokens (see Design System)
- Sprint plans and changelog noise

## Muscular America is

- A **practical fitness publishing platform**
- A system for making training concepts **understandable and usable**
- A platform spanning **books, tools, articles, and education**
- Accessible to **beginning, returning, and experienced** trainees
- Grounded in **clarity rather than hype**
- Focused on **durable principles** rather than trend chasing

## Publications and products in scope

1. **Muscular America** — central platform and website  
2. **Muscular America Field Guide** — compact, browsable gym and training guide  
3. **Simply Fit** — future accessible fitness publication or book line  
4. **Calculators** — strength, protein, calories, macros, waist-to-height, lean mass  
5. **Courses**  
6. **Community resources**  
7. **Newsletter**  
8. **Articles and reference material**  
9. Future **downloadable products** (print-ready or digital) when approved

## Muscular America is not

- Medical care
- Individualized diagnosis
- A replacement for qualified professional guidance
- A bodybuilding-only platform
- A motivational-content mill
- A collection of disconnected fitness posts
- A visual or editorial sibling brand of other publishing properties that may share operational methods

## Audience

People who train — or want to train — in real gyms and real lives: beginners, returners, and experienced trainees who prefer clear rationale over noise.

## Success measures (founding)

- Content can be reused across web, print, courses, and tools without duplication chaos
- Publications keep independent identities while sharing infrastructure
- Calculators state assumptions and never pose as diagnoses
- Documentation remains the authority for change

## Governance note

If a proposed feature conflicts with this charter, the charter wins until the Founder revises it deliberately and records the decision.
